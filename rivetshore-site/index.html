No. The pilot is read-only. We do not submit forms, make purchases, sign in, or modify production content.

You still receive a coverage report describing what was checked and what worked. Finding zero defects is a legitimate outcome.

No. This is a bounded marketing-site review. It does not certify accessibility, performance, security, browser compatibility, or a defect-free launch.

We confirm your scope and capacity first, then provide agreed payment instructions. No credit-card details are collected directly on this website.
